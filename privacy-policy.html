Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Welcome to NEXVION AI Course ("we", "our", or "us"). We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting the personal data you share with us through our mobile application and web services.

1. Information We Collect

When you use the NEXVION AI Course application, we may collect the following information:

  • Account Information: Name, email address, and profile credentials provided during registration.
  • Learning Progress: Course progression, quiz completions, assignment submissions, and certificate verifications.
  • Device and Usage Information: Device model, operating system version, app performance analytics, and diagnostic logs to enhance stability.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely for:

  • Providing access to curriculum, courses, and interactive AI learning modules.
  • Issuing and verifying verified certificates of completion.

  • Improving platform stability, security, and user experience.

3. Data Protection and Security

We implement strict security measures, including HTTPS encryption and secure database controls via Firebase and Google Cloud infrastructure, to safeguard your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, or alteration.

4. Third-Party Services

Our application utilizes trusted third-party infrastructure providers:

  • Google Firebase: For secure authentication and encrypted cloud database management.
  • Payment Gateways: For encrypted, PCI-DSS compliant checkout transactions. We never store payment card credentials on our servers.

5. Children's Privacy

Our platform does not knowingly collect personal identifiable information from children under the age of 13 without verified parental consent.

6. Your Rights

You have the right to request access to your data, update inaccuracies, or request account deletion at any time by contacting our support team.
