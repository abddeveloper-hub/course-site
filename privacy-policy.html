01. Privacy Policy Data Protection

Last updated: October 2026. NEXVION AI Academy ("we", "our", or "us") respects your privacy and is dedicated to securing student data across mobile and web platforms.

Information Collected:

  • Learner Identification: Name, authenticated email, and cohort badge IDs.
  • Curriculum Progress: Real-time lesson completions, quiz outcomes, capstone repository links, and certificate verifications.
  • Telemetry & Audit Logs: Cryptographic token exchanges, device platform signatures, and access timestamps for academic verification.

Information Usage: Student data is utilized exclusively for personalized learning roadmaps, peer capstone reviews, authenticated certificate issuance, and crucial cohort scheduling updates. We do not sell learner data.

02. Terms & Conditions Student Agreement

By registering, accessing curriculum repositories, or enrolling in any NEXVION AI tier, you agree to these Terms of Service.

  • Curriculum License: All proprietary course materials, prompt templates, agent frameworks, and video walkthroughs are licensed for individual learner education and may not be redistributed or resold.

03. Academic Honor Code Authentic Engineering

NEXVION AI teaches mastery of autonomous AI tools, modern language models, and agentic workflows. Ethical, transparent AI engineering is foundational to our community.

The NEXVION Builder Pledge: "I commit to understanding every line of code generated with AI assistance, architecting systems with transparency, and delivering capstone projects that represent my authentic understanding."
  • Attribution: Capstone submissions utilizing synthetic datasets, fine-tuned weights, or external open-source APIs must declare dependencies clearly in the project README.
  • Peer Reviews: Learners participate in respectful, constructive peer critiques during weekly cohort code walks.
  • Certificate Authenticity: Credentials issued by NEXVION AI attest to rigorous hands-on project completion. Falsifying submission artifacts results in immediate revocation.

04. 30-Seat Cohort Policy Capacity Guarantee

To preserve high-impact mentor interaction, code reviews, and live architectural design reviews, each learning batch is strictly capped at 30 active learners.

  • Waitlist Prioritization: When an active batch reaches 30 seats, new enrollments are automatically routed to the prioritized waitlist for the subsequent cohort.
  • Dedicated Mentorship Ratio: Capped batches guarantee that every student receives personalized code reviews on capstones from lead mentors.
  • Seat Reservation: Priority reservations are held for 48 hours following batch unlock announcements before releasing to open waitlists.

05. Security Overview Zero Trust Security

NEXVION AI utilizes an enterprise-grade, defense-in-depth security infrastructure across all web, Android, and backend services.

  • End-to-End Encryption: All client-server communications operate strictly over TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption.
  • Role-Based Access Control (RBAC): Administrative operations utilize granular role authorization (Owner, Super Admin, Academic Director, Student Manager) with complete immutable audit logs.
  • Payment PCI-DSS Compliance: Payment processing is handled via tokenized secure gateways. No raw payment credentials ever touch our application databases.
  • Credential Verification: Public certificate validation is verified against cryptographically stamped serial IDs to prevent credential tampering.
